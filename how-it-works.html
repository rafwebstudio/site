How it works

Four steps, all by email.

Buying a website shouldn't feel like a gamble, or eat up your day with meetings. You get a free draft of your site before you decide anything, and everything happens in writing.

Tell us about your business

Fill in the short form: your business name, services, contact details and anything you'd like on the site. It's free and there's no obligation.

Get your free draft

Using your real services and details, we design a free 3-page draft of your site and email you a private link. Click through it on your phone or computer, in your own time.

Pick your package and pay online

Like your draft? Choose the package that suits you and pay securely online by card. Not for you? Just say no thanks and you owe nothing.

We finish it and go live

Send any changes you'd like: words, photos, colours, extra pages. We finish your site, connect your domain and put it live.

After launch

Stay looked after

Optional care plans keep your site secure, backed up and up to date. Need a change later? Just email us.

What we need from you

Not much at all

The order form asks for the basics. We also work from what's already public, like your current website and business listings. If you'd like, you can also send us:

Your services

What you offer, and any prices you're happy to show.

A few photos

Your shop, your team or your work. If you don't have any, we'll use suitable licensed stock photos.

Your details

Phone, email, suburb and opening hours, so customers can reach you.
