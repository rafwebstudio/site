How it works

Four steps, all by email.

Buying a website shouldn't feel like a gamble, or eat up your day with meetings. You see a working preview of yours before you pay, and everything happens in writing.

Pick a package and send your details

Choose a package and fill in the order form: your business name, services, contact details and anything you want on the site.

We build your site

Using your real services and details, we build your site and email you a private preview link. Click through it on your phone or computer, in your own time.

Request changes by email

Reply with anything you'd like changed: words, photos, colours, pages. We make the changes and send you the updated link.

Pay and go live

Happy with it? Pay the invoice (50% to go ahead, 50% at launch) and we connect your domain and put your site live. Not for you? Just say no thanks and you owe nothing.

After launch

Stay looked after

Optional care plans keep your site secure, backed up and up to date. Need a change later? Just email us.

What we need from you

Not much at all

The order form asks for the basics. We also work from what's already public, like your current website and business listings. If you'd like, you can also send us:

Your services

What you offer, and any prices you're happy to show.

A few photos

Your shop, your team or your work. If you don't have any, we'll use suitable licensed stock photos.

Your details

Phone, email, suburb and opening hours, so customers can reach you.
