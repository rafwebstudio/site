Privacy

Privacy policy

The short version: we only use your details to contact you about your website, and we never sell or share them.

Who we are

Raf Web Studio is run by Raf Verheyen (ABN 51 838 957 009), based in North Epping NSW. You can contact us by email at raf@rafwebstudio.com.au.

What we collect

When you order or contact us, we collect what you send: usually your name, business name, email, phone number, suburb, website, services and message. This website has no tracking cookies and no advertising trackers.

How we use it

  • To reply to your order or enquiry and build your preview
  • To deliver and support your website, if you become a client
  • To send invoices and keep the business records the law requires

We don't sell, rent or share your information with anyone for marketing.

Emails from us

Sometimes we email a business about its website, using an address the business has published for enquiries. Every email says who we are and how to reach us. If you don't want to hear from us, reply “no thanks” or email raf@rafwebstudio.com.au and we'll stop straight away, and always within 5 business days.

Storage and access

Your information is kept in our email and business accounts with reputable providers. You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you at any time by emailing us.

Questions or complaints

Email raf@rafwebstudio.com.au and we'll respond within 30 days. If you're not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
