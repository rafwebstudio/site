FAQ

Questions, answered

Can't see yours here? Email us and we'll reply in writing.

Do I need to get on a call?

No. There are no sales calls. You order through our online form, we email you a preview link, and you request changes by email. Everything is in writing, so you can reply whenever suits you.

Do I pay before I see anything?

No. We design a free draft of your site first. You only pay once you've seen your finished site and decided to put it live.

What do I need to send you?

Your business name, current website (if you have one), your services, phone, email and suburb, plus anything you'd like included. Photos are welcome but optional. The order form covers it all.

What if I don't like it?

Just reply by email. You can ask for changes, or say no thanks and that's the end of it. You owe nothing and we take the preview down.

How do payments work?

One payment, made securely online by card once you're happy with your finished site and before it goes live. No deposit. All prices are in Australian dollars.

How long does it take?

A one-page site is live within 7 days of you saying go. Bigger sites depend on how many pages and how quickly we get your content, and we'll confirm a date with you by email before we start.

Who owns my website and domain?

You do. Your domain name is registered in your name, so you always stay in control of it.

Do I need a care plan?

A care plan is optional. Essentials covers hosting, small edits and email support. Plus includes up to 1 hour of changes each month, so you don't have to think about any of it.

Can you work with my booking system?

Yes. The 5-page site can link to the booking system you already use, such as Fresha, Cliniko or HotDoc. The online bookings package sets bookings up for you.

Do you set up email?

We don't host email ourselves. We'll point you to a reliable provider like Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 and send you clear steps to connect it to your domain.

Where are you based?

North Epping, in Sydney's north-west. We work with small businesses right across greater Sydney, all by email, so it doesn't matter which suburb you're in.

I run a health practice. Is that a problem?

Not at all. We keep your site in line with AHPRA advertising rules: no patient testimonials, no “best” claims and no promised outcomes.

Ready for a website that works?

Tell us about your business and we'll design a free 3-page draft of your new site. You only pay if you love it and want it live.
