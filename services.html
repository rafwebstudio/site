The fine print

Straightforward terms

Preview first

We email you a preview link of your site before you pay anything.

Pay after your free preview

One card payment online, once you've seen your free preview and picked a package.

You own your domain

Your domain, content and Google listing stay in your name, so you're always in control.

30 days to send your content

If we haven't received your details and content within 30 days of payment, we'll finish and launch your site using the content from your approved preview, and you can send updates later.

Refunds

Because you see and approve your free preview before paying, we don't offer change-of-mind refunds once work has started. This doesn't affect your rights under Australian Consumer Law.

What we don’t do: paid ads, social media management, video, email hosting or IT support. We’re happy to point you to someone who does. Nobody can honestly promise a top spot on Google, so we don’t.
